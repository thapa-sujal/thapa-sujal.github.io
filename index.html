About

Research interests

Surrogate Modeling & ML Computational Fluid Dynamics Hypersonic Aerodynamics Scientific Machine Learning

Hi! I'm 23 right now, but I keep getting older all the time. My work primarily focuses on developing efficient metamodels for conventionally expensive simulations. The goal, however, is to contribute towards advancing the understanding of things, rather than just being able to approximate solutions faster.

The nerdy parts aside, I'm a pretty chill dude. I like paying my dues on the guitar, I can bench-press an adult giant panda or a young lion, and, to the detriment of my relationships, I enjoy reading philosophy (mostly ethics, and only the Greeks at that).
